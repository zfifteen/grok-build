> Trace two to three waves of consequences: and then what?

# Method
1. State the proposed action or default path.
2. Wave 1: immediate effects.
3. Wave 2: responses, adaptations, load shifts.
4. Wave 3: longer-term or systemic blowback.
5. Flag where a fix likely creates a new problem.

# Stop rules
Do not endorse a first-order win that fails wave-2/3 scrutiny without labeling residual risk.

# Artifact details
Waves 1–3 and reversal risks.
