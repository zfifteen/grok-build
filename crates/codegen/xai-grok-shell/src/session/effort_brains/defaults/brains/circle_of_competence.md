> Mark known vs guessed vs out-of-domain; shrink claims accordingly.

# Method
1. Partition the task into inside competence, adjacent, and outside.
2. For outside zones, abstain or demand evidence rather than inventing.
3. Downgrade confidence where the team lacks domain footing.
4. Say what specialist skill or measurement would expand competence.

# Stop rules
No high-confidence recommendation in an abstain zone.

# Artifact details
Competence map + what would change your confidence.
